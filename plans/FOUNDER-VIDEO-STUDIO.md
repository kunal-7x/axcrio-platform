# 🎬 Your Video Studio — make a ready-to-post ad video in 1 minute

Your Video Studio is **LIVE**. It turns a one-line idea into a real, vertical, ready-for-
WhatsApp-and-ads video — voiceover, on-screen captions and all — for **almost zero cost**
(no expensive AI-video key needed; it uses your own voice engine).

## How to make a video (5 clicks)

1. Open the panel: **panel.famit.in** → log in.
2. In the left menu, go to **Creative Studio → Video Studio**.
3. Pick a **campaign** from the dropdown (e.g. *Codename Joy 3.0*). The studio reads your
   business + product + offer so the video is on-message.
4. Leave the tier on **Composite** (the default — free of any paid AI key) and the shape on
   **9:16** (vertical, best for Reels/Status/Stories).
5. In the big box, type what you want. Example:
   > *"30-second reel for a 2 BHK at Codename Joy 3.0 — spacious balconies, club amenities,
   > ready to move. Book a free site visit."*
6. Click **Generate**.

## What happens next

- The AI writes the ad **script**, speaks it in a natural **voice** (Hindi/Hinglish/regional),
  adds **on-screen captions**, and renders a real **MP4**.
- You'll see the clip appear in **Generation** and, within a few moments, in your
  **Library** (the same place your images live — flip the **Images ↔ Videos** toggle at the
  top to see your videos).
- Click the video to **play it**. It has picture **and** sound.

## Then use it

- From the video (or the Library), **attach it to a WhatsApp message** or to a **Meta/Google
  ad** the same way you attach an image. It's a normal asset in your library now.

## Good to know

- **Cost:** the default "Composite" tier costs almost nothing — it uses your existing voice
  engine (Sarvam), not a pricey AI-video service. You only pay for premium tiers if you
  deliberately switch to **AI motion** or **Premium** (those ask for approval first).
- **Each Generate makes its own video automatically** — no waiting on anyone. Type, click,
  done.
- **It won't disturb your calls.** The video render runs completely separately from your live
  AI calling agent — your voice earner is never touched.

That's it. Type an idea → **Generate** → watch it render → it lands in your Library, ready to
send on WhatsApp or run as an ad.
