## What's blocked and why (in plain words)

Right now, our system **cannot send WhatsApp messages to your leads.** Meta (the company
that owns WhatsApp) has put a hold on the account. There are a few separate reasons
stacked on top of each other, and we need to clear them one by one:

1. **No working payment method** on the WhatsApp account — this is the **#1 reason** sending

2. **The business isn't "verified"** with Meta yet — they need to confirm we're a real

3. **The WhatsApp "display name" hasn't been approved**, which keeps us capped at only

4. **Customer replies aren't being delivered to our system**, so even when someone writes

You can fix all four yourself by clicking through the steps below. **Do them in order** —
step 1 is the most urgent. Take your time; there's nothing you can break here.

## ✅ STEP 1 — Add or fix the payment method (DO THIS FIRST)

This is the single most important step. Without a working card, **nothing else matters** —
the account stays blocked.

1. Open your web browser and go to **https://business.facebook.com** and log in.
2. Make sure the correct business is selected at the **top-left** (if you run more than one,
   click the name at the top-left and pick the right one — the one tied to the **MedFlow /
   +91 97550 40013** account, or whichever is the real Famit account).
3. Find and click the **gear icon ⚙️ ("Settings"** or **"Business Settings")**. It's usually
   in the **bottom-left corner** or under the menu on the left side.
4. A settings menu opens on the **left**. Click **"Accounts"** to expand it, then click
   **"WhatsApp Accounts"**.
5. In the list, **click the WhatsApp account** you want to fix (e.g. the MedFlow one).
6. On the right side / top of that account's page, look for **"Billing & Payments"**,
   **"Payment settings"**, or simply a **"Payment methods"** tab. Click it.
   - *Tip:* if you can't find it here, you can also reach the same place via
     **WhatsApp Manager → Settings → Payment settings**. Both lead to the same screen.
7. Click the blue **"Add Payment Method"** button.
8. It will ask for:
   - Your **business location** (country) and **currency** — pick India / INR.
   - **Payment method** — choose **Credit or Debit card**.
   - **Card details** — the **name exactly as printed on the card**, the **16-digit card
     number**, the **expiry (MM/YY)**, and the **CVV**.
9. Click **Save / Add**. Your bank may send an **OTP to your phone** to approve the card —
   **enter it** if asked. (If you skip this OTP, the card will look "added" but won't actually
   work — so don't skip it.)

### 👀 How to confirm Step 1 worked
- Go back to the **Payment settings** screen. The card should now be listed, **with no red
  warning** and **no "Action required"** banner.
- The account status should no longer say things like *"payment method error"* or
  *"business-initiated conversations are blocked"*.
- If you still see a red warning: the card was likely **declined, expired, or not OTP-verified**.
  Try a **different card** (one you know is active with international payments enabled), and
  re-do steps 7–9. A failed or unverified card is the usual culprit.

## ✅ STEP 2 — Complete Business Verification

Meta needs to confirm we're a real, registered business. This can take a **few days** for
them to review, so start it now even while you work on the other steps.

1. Still at **https://business.facebook.com**, open **Settings / Business Settings** again
   (the gear ⚙️).
2. In the left menu, look for **"Security Center"** (sometimes called
   **"Business verification"**). Click it.
3. You'll see a **"Business verification"** section. Click the **"Start verification"** button.
4. Meta will walk you through a form. You'll typically be asked for:
   - **Business legal name, address, and phone number** — fill these in exactly as they
     appear on your official papers.
   - **A document proving the business is real** — usually **one** of:
     business registration / incorporation certificate, **GST certificate**, business
     **PAN**, a business license, or **business tax document**.
   - Sometimes **proof of address** — e.g. a recent **utility bill** (electricity / phone)
     or bank statement in the business's name.
5. Upload clear, **un-edited** photos or PDFs. Make sure the **name and address on the
   documents match exactly** what you typed in the form (mismatches are the #1 reason
   verification gets rejected).
6. Submit.

### 👀 How to confirm Step 2 worked
- Right after submitting, the status in **Security Center** changes to
  **"Verification in progress"** / **"Pending"**. That's correct — now you wait.
- Meta usually replies within a **few days** (sometimes faster). When done it will say
  **"Verified"** ✅.
- If it says **"Not verified" / "Rejected"**, open Security Center, click the rejection
  message to see **why**, fix that one thing (usually a blurry document or a name mismatch),
  and **re-submit**. Send me the rejection reason and I'll help.

## ✅ STEP 4 — Turn on incoming-message delivery (so replies reach our AI)

This connects WhatsApp to our server so that when a customer **replies**, our AI actually
**receives it** and responds. This one happens on a slightly different Meta website — the
**developer dashboard**.

1. Go to **https://developers.facebook.com** and log in (same Facebook login).
2. Click **"My Apps"** at the top-right, then **click our app** in the list.
   *(If you see more than one app and aren't sure which, send me the list of app names and
   I'll tell you which one.)*
3. In the app's **left menu**, click **"WhatsApp"**, then click **"Configuration"**.
4. You'll see a **"Webhook"** box. Click **"Edit"** and enter:
   - **Callback URL:**
     ```
     https://panel.famit.in/api/whatsapp/inbound
     ```
   - **Verify token:** ⚠️ **Don't worry about this one now.** It's a secret value set on our
     server. **When you reach this exact step, message me ("ask Claude for the verify
     token") and I'll give it to you to paste in.** Do not guess or invent it.
5. With both fields filled, click **"Verify and Save"**.
   - If it's correct, the box closes with a small **green tick / "success"**. If it shows an
     error, the token was wrong — ping me and we'll re-check it together.
6. Now, just **below** the Webhook box, find **"Webhook fields"** and click **"Manage"**.
7. A list of fields appears. Find the row called **"messages"** and click
   **"Subscribe"** next to it. (If there's a **"messages_template_status_update"** row,
   subscribing to that too is fine, but **"messages" is the must-have**.)
8. Click **Done**.

### 👀 How to confirm Step 4 worked
- The Webhook box shows your callback URL **https://panel.famit.in/api/whatsapp/inbound**
  with a **"Connected" / green** status.
- In the **Webhook fields** list, the **"messages"** row shows **"Subscribed"** (a green
  tick or the word *Subscribed* instead of a *Subscribe* button).
- Tell me once it shows subscribed, and I'll send a test reply to confirm our AI receives it.
