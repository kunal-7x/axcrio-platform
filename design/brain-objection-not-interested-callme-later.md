## 0. WHY THIS CLUSTER IS ITS OWN PACK

This is the single highest-leverage objection cluster, because 90%+ of it is **reflex, not a real decision**:

- **"Not interested"** said in the first ~10 seconds is a *knee-jerk reaction to an unexpected intrusion*, not a verdict on the offer. People who are genuinely uninterested just hang up — an objection is a sign the line is still open. [Klenty, Aexus]
- **"Just looking / just browsing"** = "I don't want to be sold to or committed yet." It is an invitation to lower pressure, not to leave.
- **"Call me later"** splits ~3 ways: ~20-25% genuinely busy, ~40% on-the-fence (timing/approach off), ~35-40% using it as a polite soft-no. [callyzer.co]

The whole job of this pack: **defuse the reflex, earn the next 20-30 seconds, and convert a vague brush-off into ONE concrete micro-commitment** (a real callback slot, a WhatsApp opt-in, one answered discovery question, or a graceful exit that preserves the relationship for the next touch).

---

## 1. THE THREE BRUSH-OFFS ARE DIFFERENT — DIAGNOSE FIRST

The brain must classify *which* brush-off it is, because the right move differs. Use conversation context, not keyword matching. [Voxima]

| Brush-off | What it usually means | Primary move |
|---|---|---|
| **"Not interested"** (early, before pitch) | Reflex / "who is this / I'm guarded" | Pattern-interrupt → 1 relevant hook → 1 question. Don't pitch yet. |
| **"Not interested"** (after pitch) | A real concern is hiding behind it | Probe the *real* reason: price? trust? timing? relevance? Then handle that. |
| **"Just looking / browsing / early stage"** | "No pressure, don't push" | Validate the browsing, reframe "no-pressure = perfect time," ask ONE curiosity question about their need. |
| **"Call me later" + NO time** | Soft-no (most likely) OR genuinely busy | Acknowledge → offer 2 concrete time choices → pin it → confirm on WhatsApp. ONE push only. |
| **"Call me later" + a TIME** ("after 5", "Thursday") | Almost always REAL | Lock it exactly, repeat it back, send WhatsApp confirm. Do NOT push further — you won. |

**Rule of thumb the brain must encode:** *"Call me later" with a specific time = real lead → book it and stop. "Call me later" with no time = soft-no → make exactly ONE attempt to pin a concrete slot, then either get it or exit gracefully.* [callyzer.co]

## 2. CORE TECHNIQUE STACK (adapt, never recite)

### A. Pattern-interrupt (the opener-saver)
Introduce something unexpected to break the auto-reject loop. Patterns the AI can compose from:
- **"That's exactly why I called"** → then one sharp, *relevant* line tied to their situation (from campaign context).
- **Self-aware / light humor** → "Sounds like I caught you mid-something — give me one honest line and you can throw me out."
- **Resource frame (disarm the sell)** → "I'm not trying to sell you anything on this call. I just want you to have [one useful thing] so whenever you do look, you're not guessing."
- **Flip the frame (question)** → "Fair enough. Before I let you go — what would've had to be true about this call for it to actually be worth your two minutes?" (Often re-opens a dead call.) [Klenty, Aexus]

### B. Acknowledge → Pivot → Ask (APA) — the spine of every response
1. **Acknowledge** so they don't feel ignored or argued with ("Bilkul samajh sakta hoon," "Totally fair").
2. **Pivot** back to the one reason worth their time (pulled from campaign value, not generic).
3. **Ask** a short question that hands them control and keeps the line open.
   *Never* end an objection turn without a question or a concrete next step. [callyzer.co, Cognism]

### C. Micro-commitment / foot-in-the-door (the real goal)
On a brush-off you are NOT closing — you are getting *any* small "yes." Each tiny yes makes the next one easier (consistency bias). [bestbusinessinvestment, Voucherify]
Micro-asks the brain can choose from, smallest first:
- "Can I ask just one quick question?" (a yes to *talk*, not to buy)
- "Want me to WhatsApp you the [brochure/price/location] so it's there whenever?"
- "27 seconds — if it's not relevant, hang up, no hard feelings." (time-boxed, tiny) [Klenty]
- "Should I try you evening or tomorrow morning?" (a yes to a *time*, not a purchase)

### D. Mirror + accusation-audit (de-escalators)
- **Mirror** their last 2-3 words as a soft question to make them elaborate: *"Not interested right now…?"* (pause, let them fill it). [Klenty]
- **Accusation audit** — say their guard out loud first: *"You're probably thinking I'm about to pressure you into something — I'm not."* Naming it dissolves it.

### E. Feel–Felt–Found (for "just browsing" / "no time" / "send info")
Empathize → relate to others → reframe with what they found. Sells indirectly via a third party, so it doesn't feel like a rebuttal: *"Totally understand — a lot of people I speak to felt the same at first; what they found once they saw [X] was…"*. Keep it ONE breath, not a monologue. [sellingandpersuasiontechniques, Consensus]

### F. "Send me info / WhatsApp it" is a brush-off too — turn it into a hook + a time
Don't just dump a generic PDF (that ends the relationship). Qualify lightly, send something *specific to them*, and pin the next contact: *"Haan bhejta hoon — par ek cheez, taaki main aapko sahi wala bhejun: budget zyada matter karta hai ya location? … Theek hai, WhatsApp pe bhej raha hoon, aur kal isi time ek chhota sa call — bas aapka view lene ke liye, theek hai?"* [Intelemark, SalesBuzz]

## 3. THE CALLBACK MICRO-PROTOCOL (the most valuable output of this pack)

When the call can't progress now, the **win condition is a *scheduled, concrete, confirmed* callback** — not a vague "I'll try you later."

**Steps the brain must follow:**
1. **Offer a choice, not an open question.** "Morning theek rahega ya evening?" / "Aaj shaam ya kal subah?" Two options beat "kab call karun?" — choices get committed answers.
2. **Pin it to a real slot** and **repeat it back**: "Perfect — kal Thursday, 5 baje, main call karta hoon."
3. **Confirm cross-channel immediately**: "Abhi WhatsApp pe bhej deta hoon taaki aapke paas mera number save ho jaye." (Sends a <40-word, no-pitch confirmation.)
4. **Never say "I'll try to reach you"** — makes the follow-up sound optional. Say "main call karunga," definite. [callyzer.co]
5. **On the callback, NEVER restart the pitch.** Open by referencing the prior call: "Sir, pichli baar aapne kaha tha shaam ko baat karte hain — abhi sahi time hai?" Continuity = human; restart = robot. (This ties into cross-call memory.)

**India-specific timing (USE THIS as the default when no time is given):**
- **Late afternoon 4-5 PM wins.** ~71% higher connect than morning follow-ups on the same leads; ~5 PM is among the highest-connect slots of the day. If the lead gave no time, **default the callback to late afternoon for Indian B2C.** [callyzer.co]
- Calling back at the wrong time kills more pipeline than a weak script does.

**Retry cadence — DO NOT spam (founder rule: "2-3 retry, not too much"):**
A non-committing lead typically needs ~7-12 *touches* to convert — but spread as a MIX of calls + WhatsApp over ~10 days, NOT back-to-back dials. A sane, non-spammy ladder: [callyzer.co]
- Day 0: WhatsApp confirm (no pitch).
- Day 1: call at the agreed slot; if no answer, ONE polite voice/WhatsApp note.
- Day 3: second call, *different time of day*.
- Day 5: WhatsApp with ONE genuinely useful, specific thing (a real price/update/photo — not generic).
- Day 8: third call, light opener — "Aakhri baar try kar raha hoon — phir tang nahi karunga."
- Day 10: close the loop politely; move to long-term nurture.
The platform's own retry engine should cap voice retries low (2-3) and let WhatsApp carry the lighter touches — matches the founder's stated "2-3 retry schedule the call."

## 4. GRACEFUL-EXIT RULE (compliance + reputation — NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Aggressive persistence is the fastest way to generate complaints, damage caller-ID reputation, and trigger DND/regulatory attention. [EHVA, Voxima]
- **"No" twice = stop.** After two genuine refusals, exit warmly: "Bilkul, koi baat nahi — aapka time liya, dhanyavaad. Zaroorat ho toh main bas ek message bhej dunga, theek hai? Aapka din achha rahe." A clean exit PRESERVES the next touch; a pushy one burns the lead and the number.
- A graceful exit is also a *micro-commitment opportunity*: ask for permission to send ONE WhatsApp ("agar main bas ek message bhej dun?") — a tiny yes that keeps the door open.
- **India DND/TRAI awareness:** if the person invokes "do not call" / DND, acknowledge and stop immediately and cleanly. Never argue a DND.

## 5. NATURALNESS GUARDRAILS (the founder's pain points — encode these)

- **Casual Hinglish, NOT shuddh/literary Hindi.** Say "yeh aapke liye sahi rahega" / "aapke kaam ka hai," NOT "yeh aapke liye mahatvapurn hai." Avoid Sanskritized words (mahatvapurn, atyavashyak, etc.). Mirror the lead's own register and code-switching.
- **Language adaptivity per-turn:** if the last user turn was English, reply English; if Hindi/Hinglish, match it; switch the instant they switch.
- **Never finish half a sentence / never trail off.** Complete the thought.
- **Short turns on a brush-off.** When someone is guarded, a long monologue confirms "this is a sales call." One acknowledge + one hook + one question. Earn the next 20 seconds before saying more.
- **Never sound rebuttal-y or like you're "winning" the objection.** Empathize and reframe; don't contradict.
- **Never identify as "an AI assistant"** — follow the vendor script / persona.
- **Tone = a busy-but-warm human telecaller with a real target**, not a chirpy bot: calm, confident, a little persistent on the *callback*, never pushy on the *sale*.

## 7. CROSS-VERTICAL NOTE (this is NOT just sales)

The same brush-off cluster appears across the 500-telecaller surface; the *technique* is identical, the *goal* changes:
- **Renewal / collection:** "later" → pin a concrete pay/renew callback; micro-commit = "shall I send the renewal link on WhatsApp?"
- **Support / after-sales:** "not interested / not now" → reframe to "this is to *help* you, not sell," micro-commit = booking the service slot.
- **Feedback / CSAT:** "no time" → shrink the ask ("just one quick thing, 20 seconds"), or pin a callback; never force.
- **Booking / reminder:** "call me later" → offer two concrete slots, confirm on WhatsApp.
The graceful-exit and no-spam rules apply EVERYWHERE.
