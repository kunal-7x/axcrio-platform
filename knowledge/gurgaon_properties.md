# Gurgaon Property Inventory

Use this file to shortlist Gurgaon/Gurugram residential options during calls. Treat every price, possession timeline, amenity and availability note as indicative. Do not promise exact pricing, inventory, discounts, legal status, booking terms, or possession dates. Always say the sales team will confirm live details.

Callback number rule: use the configured sales callback number from the agent. If a callback number is not available, collect caller name, phone number, budget, preferred area, and callback time.

Site visit rule for every project: site visits are by appointment only. First qualify budget, preferred location, BHK, self-use or investment, possession timeline, and loan requirement. Then offer a site visit slot and say the sales team will confirm address, current price, availability, and documents needed.

If the caller says "best property" or "Gurgaon flat", ask budget first. Do not immediately recommend a project.

Suggested first question: "Aapka approx budget kya hai, aur Gurgaon mein kaunsi side prefer kar rahe hain - Dwarka Expressway, Golf Course Extension, SPR/New Gurgaon, ya Manesar side?"

## Budget Buckets

- Under 1.5 Cr: compact options, resale, floors, or lower-ticket projects; do not push luxury inventory.
- 1.5 Cr to 3 Cr: New Gurgaon, Sector 80/86/89, Manesar, selected Dwarka Expressway options.
- 3 Cr to 5 Cr: Emaar, Signature Global, Birla, Ashiana, M3M and selected Dwarka Expressway/Golf Course Extension options.
- 5 Cr to 10 Cr: DLF Privana, Max Estate, BPTP, Experion, Elan, Signature Global luxury, Golf Course Extension.
- Above 10 Cr: Elan The Statement, Godrej Sora, Delphine Central Park Estates, ultra-luxury DLF/Central Park options.
