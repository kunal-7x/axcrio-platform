The Problem

The revenue loop is broken between the click and the cash — and nobody owns it.

A lead is worth most in the first 5 minutes. The telecaller is asleep, on another call, or gone by 9pm. 200 ad leads → a handful get called. The ad budget keeps buying form-fillers who never answer.

  • 1Speed-to-lead fails. Human telecallers don't scale, churn constantly, and never follow up across channels.
  • 2The ads learn the wrong thing. SMBs feed Meta/Google junk signals — "form submitted" — so the money optimizes for clickers, not buyers.
  • 3Two expensive teams, zero loop. A telecaller team and a marketing team — neither closes the loop back to the ad that started it.
The bleed: every wasted ad rupee buys the wrong customer, and every uncalled lead is revenue that quietly walks away.
The Solution

An AI workforce that runs the entire revenue loop.

Ad → lead → AI voice call → WhatsApp follow-up → appointment → revenue — under hard safety (PIN · approval · audit) and twice-enforced tenant isolation. Live today.
