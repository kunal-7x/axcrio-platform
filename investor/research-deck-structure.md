# RESEARCH — VC-Grade Pitch-Deck Structure (Famit / Axcrio Seed Raise)

> **Purpose:** the canonical slide blueprint + per-slide guidance + design rules for an
> INVESTOR PITCH DECK (raise money / sell the company as an investment) — NOT a sales
> proposal. Synthesized from the YC seed-deck guide, the Sequoia template, a16z/2025–26
> AI-SaaS guidance, and a 2026 analysis of famous funded decks. Sources at the bottom.
>
> **Context for this deck:** vertical-AI / AI-SaaS, seed stage, real LIVE product +
> early proof points (96 calls, 8 campaigns, billing meter, 18/18 isolation probes),
> flagship moat = the **Revenue-Truth Signal Loop** (post-click conversation layer +
> cross-tenant data network effect). The deck must read a16z/Sequoia-restrained, big
> numbers, one idea per slide, minimal text.

---

## 0. THE BIG TRUTHS THAT SHAPE EVERYTHING (read first)

1. **The 2:14 skim test.** A first-pass VC review averages ~2 min 14 sec. The **first 4
   slides get ~60% of total attention.** Decks > 15 slides see ~40% lower engagement.
   → Front-load the punch. Title + Problem + Solution + (Why-Now or Traction) must land
   the entire thesis on their own.
2. **One idea per slide.** Every slide makes exactly ONE point, stated in the headline.
   If the headline alone tells the story, the slide works. Body = evidence for the headline.
3. **The MOAT slide now does ~30% of the underwriting work** for any AI-adjacent pitch —
   partners debate this slide above all others. For us, this is the make-or-break slide:
   the Revenue-Truth Signal Loop is the whole reason this is a venture-scale company, not
   a tool. Give it room; make it a diagram, not a paragraph.
4. **Big numbers, minimal text, demo over description.** Lead with numbers visible at a
   glance. Show the product (screenshot / loop diagram), never an architecture diagram.
5. **Specificity kills generic.** Bottom-up market math beats top-down TAM. "Surgical"
   why-now (a shift in the last 6–12 months) beats macro tailwinds. Named, structural
   moat beats "proprietary AI." Shipping credentials beat "ex-Google."
6. **Never fabricate.** Every metric is real or tagged roadmap. Investors diligence;
   one inflated number kills trust in all of them. Our edge is that the product is LIVE.

## 1. CANONICAL SLIDE ORDER (the blueprint)

The consensus seed AI-SaaS order, merged from YC + Sequoia + a16z + the famous-deck
analysis. Target **12–14 slides** in the core deck (+ optional appendix). This is the
recommended order for THIS deck:

| # | Slide | One job |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | **Title / One-liner** | Name + logo + the single sentence that positions the company |
| 2 | **Problem** | The painful, specific, expensive problem — a real scenario, not abstract pain |
| 3 | **Solution** | What we built, in as few words as possible — the "aha" |
| 4 | **Why Now** | The surgical shift (last 6–12 mo) that makes this possible/inevitable NOW |
| 5 | **Product / How it works** | The closed loop, shown — screenshot/diagram, demo energy |
| 6 | **The Moat / Why we win** | ⭐ The Revenue-Truth Signal Loop + data network effect — the deciding slide |
| 7 | **Traction** | Real proof: 96 calls, 8 campaigns, billing meter, 18/18 probes, LIVE |
| 8 | **Market size** | Bottom-up: # SMBs × ACV × capture — make it big but credible |
| 9 | **Business model** | How we make money, unit economics legible in one slide |
| 10 | **Go-to-market** | How we acquire vendors cheaply + the wedge that compounds |
| 11 | **Competition** | 2×2 in an open quadrant — own the post-click conversation axis |
| 12 | **Team** | Why THIS team ships THIS — shipping credentials, founder-market fit |
| 13 | **Vision** | From AI telecaller → the AI Revenue OS / Growth OS ($500M-ARR thesis) |
| 14 | **The Ask** | How much, what it buys, the milestones it unlocks (Series-A-ready) |

> **Ordering note / a defensible variant:** if traction is the strongest asset (a LIVE
> product with real calls/campaigns is rare at seed), some VCs advise moving **Traction
> earlier (right after Solution, ~slide 4)** to capture the 60% attention window. For
> Famit the recommended call is to keep the moat→traction adjacency (slides 6–7) so the
> proof immediately backs the moat claim — but a Traction-at-4 cut is a legitimate A/B.

## 2. PER-SLIDE GUIDANCE (what each slide must say — Famit-specific)

### 1 — Title / One-liner
- Logo (white mark on dark), company name, ONE sentence. No clutter, no taglines stacked.
- Line: **"Famit — the AI Revenue Workforce. One operator, an AI team that calls, follows
  up on WhatsApp, books the appointment, and feeds real outcomes back to the ads."**
- Optionally a quiet sub-line: stage / "Seed" / "live at panel.famit.in". Confidence = whitespace.

### 2 — Problem
- A specific, expensive scenario, not "SMBs struggle with marketing." Example:
  *"A real-estate lead is worth most in the first 5 minutes. The telecaller is asleep,
  on another call, or gone by 9pm. 200 ad leads → a handful called → the ad budget keeps
  buying form-fillers who never answer."* Make the reader feel the bleed.
- The deeper structural problem: **SMBs feed Meta/Google junk signals ("form submitted")**
  so the ad money optimizes for clickers, not buyers. Telecaller + marketing teams are
  expensive, don't scale, don't follow up, and never close the loop back to the ads.
- One idea: *the revenue loop is broken between the click and the cash, and nobody owns it.*

### 3 — Solution
- The "aha" in one breath: **an AI workforce that runs the entire revenue loop** —
  Ad → Lead → AI voice CALL → WhatsApp follow-up → appointment → revenue — under hard
  safety (PIN / approval / audit) and twice-enforced tenant isolation. LIVE today.
- Show the loop as ONE picture. Resist listing features here (features come on slide 5).
- The contrast line: *"Creative tools stop at the click. We own everything after it."*

### 4 — Why Now
- Surgical, recent shifts (not "AI is big"):
  - Real-time, low-latency, **multilingual** voice agents crossed the human-quality /
    cost threshold in the last ~12–18 months (we run it live, Hinglish, ~Rs-per-call meter).
  - WhatsApp Cloud API + Meta/Google **conversions API** make outcome-fed signal loops
    possible for the first time for SMBs.
  - SMB labor cost ↑, telecaller churn, and the collapse of cookie-based attribution make
    an owned-conversation truth layer newly necessary.
- One idea: *the voice + signal-loop became technically and economically possible NOW.*

### 5 — Product / How it works
- SHOW it. Screenshot of the panel + the closed-loop diagram with one unified memory per
  lead across every hop. Demo energy beats architecture.
- Name the live engine briefly: AI Voice (Riya), WhatsApp builder, Creative Studio (real
  AI banners), Billing meter, AI Manager (voice/chat command brain), Control Layer.
- One idea: *it's a working product, not a slide — the whole loop runs today.*

### 6 — The Moat / Why we win ⭐ (THE deciding slide)
- This is where the company becomes venture-scale. Give it a clean diagram, big claim.
- **The Revenue-Truth Signal Loop:** we own the post-click conversation (voice + WhatsApp),
  so we know the **real ground truth of lead quality** — answered? qualified? booked? bought?
  We feed that back to Meta/Google as **quality-weighted conversions**, so the ad platforms
  hunt for people who **answer calls and buy**, not people who click. No creative tool,
  dashboard, or agency owns this loop end-to-end.
- Stack the compounding moats (briefly): (1) signal loop, (2) owned conversation layer,
  (3) **cross-tenant data network effect** (every vendor makes the next vendor's first
  campaign smarter — tag the live aggregate as roadmap honestly), (4) Creative-DNA
  attribute-level learning (biases winning style, never fabricates facts).
- The line VCs remember: *"The platforms decide who sees the ad. We decide what to say,
  whether the lead was real, and what to do next — and we prove it with revenue, not clicks."*
- One idea: *a post-click conversation moat + a data network effect competitors can't copy.*

### 7 — Traction
- Big numbers, glanceable, ALL REAL (never inflate):
  **96 real AI calls · 8 live campaigns · live per-call vendor billing meter · real AI
  banners generated (no-double-spend proven) · 18/18 tenant-isolation probes PASS ·
  Control Layer LIVE + enforcing in production.**
- Frame as proof of *capability and rigor*, not yet revenue scale: "the hard parts —
  real-time multilingual voice, money custody with no double-spend, twice-enforced
  multi-tenant isolation — are DONE and proven live." That de-risks the bet.
- One idea: *the technically hard, trust-critical core is already live and proven.*

### 8 — Market size
- **Bottom-up**, not a Gartner $XXXb slide. Example shape: # of target SMBs (real estate,
  salons, clinics, coaching, D2C, agencies) in India (then global) × realistic ACV
  (replaces a telecaller + a marketing hire = large, defensible willingness-to-pay) ×
  reachable share. Show the multiplication on the slide.
- Anchor ACV to the pain: we replace *people* (a telecaller team + a marketing person),
  so the budget is a salary line, not a SaaS-tool line — that's the TAM unlock.
- One idea: *every SMB that runs ads and makes calls is a customer; the math is huge AND credible.*

### 9 — Business model
- One legible slide: subscription + usage (per-call / per-credit metered via the live
  wallet) + take-rate optionality on ad-spend optimization later. Show unit economics:
  cost-per-call meter (~Rs-level), gross-margin logic, the wedge → expand motion.
- One idea: *metered usage on top of a seat/sub — margins improve as the signal loop compounds.*

### 10 — Go-to-market
- The wedge: land with the AI telecaller (sharp, immediate ROI pain), expand into the
  full loop (WhatsApp, creative, booking, signal loop). Per-vertical Industry Packs.
- Distribution: agency/white-label multi-tenant mode (Control Layer) = one agency brings
  many vendors = cheap CAC + faster cross-tenant network effect. Name the motion clearly.
- One idea: *land on the telecaller pain, expand to the OS, scale via agencies/white-label.*

### 11 — Competition
- A **2×2** placing us in an open quadrant. Suggested axes: *owns the post-click
  conversation* (voice+WhatsApp) × *closes the loop back to ad spend with real outcomes*.
  Creative/ad tools (Advantage+, PMax, "AI ad" tools) sit bottom-left; point solutions
  (Vapi/Bland voice, a WhatsApp tool) sit on one axis only. **We're alone top-right.**
- Explicit framing: *"We don't fight Advantage+/PMax on delivery — we feed them better
  signals and better creative. We win the layer they can't see."*
- One idea: *everyone owns a slice; only we own the post-click loop end-to-end.*

### 12 — Team
- Founder-market fit + shipping credentials, not titles. Emphasize: shipped a LIVE,
  hardened, multi-tenant, money-handling AI platform with proven isolation/no-double-spend
  — i.e., *this team can build and operate the hard, trust-critical thing.* 3 people max
  on the slide; rest to appendix.
- One idea: *the team that already shipped the hard part live.*

### 13 — Vision
- Zoom out: from "AI telecaller" → the **AI Revenue OS / Growth OS** — the system of record
  for SMB revenue, with a cross-tenant learning network that compounds into a $500M-ARR
  thesis. Keep aspirational but earned by the moat slide. Tag the network-effect aggregate
  as roadmap.
- One idea: *we're building the revenue operating system for every SMB that advertises.*

### 14 — The Ask
- Specific: **amount raised, runway it buys, the 3–4 milestones it unlocks** (e.g.,
  activate the dormant modules + inbound voice DID, ship the live cross-tenant signal
  aggregate, N paying vendors, $X ARR) — and state what makes us **Series-A-ready** in
  12–18 months. Use-of-funds in 3–4 buckets.
- One idea: *here's the check, here's exactly what it unlocks, here's the Series-A setup.*

> **Optional appendix (after the Ask, not counted in the 2:14):** detailed metrics, the
> full capability list (LIVE / READY-DORMANT / SOON), security/trust deep-dive (PIN
> firewall, immutable audit, FORCE-RLS, DND/DPDP), extended team, financial model.

## 3. DESIGN RULES (a16z / Sequoia restraint)

- **One idea per slide; the headline carries it.** Headline = the takeaway sentence, not
  a label ("Traction" → "The hard, trust-critical core is already live"). Body proves it.
- **Big numbers, huge.** 96 / 8 / 18-of-18 / market math set in large type; everything
  else recedes. Numbers are the heroes.
- **Minimal text.** ~Few lines per slide. If it needs a paragraph, it's two slides or an
  appendix. Dense slides consistently underperform.
- **Show, don't diagram.** Product screenshots + the loop picture > architecture diagrams.
  The moat is the one diagram worth a full slide.
- **Restraint & whitespace = confidence.** Generous margins, one accent color (Signal blue
  `#2A85FF`), clean Inter-style type, dark premium slides with the white logo mark.
  Consistent grid, consistent type scale. No clip-art, no gradients-for-gradients.
- **≤ 14 core slides.** Respect the 2:14 / 15-slide-cliff. Everything else → appendix.
- **Front-load the thesis** in slides 1–4 (the 60% attention window).
- **Every claim real or tagged roadmap.** Roadmap items visually marked (a quiet "roadmap"
  chip), never blended into live proof. Trust is the asset; protect it on every slide.
- **Printable to PDF, one slide per page** — design at 16:9, ensure each slide is a clean
  standalone page (no reliance on hover/animation to convey the point).
