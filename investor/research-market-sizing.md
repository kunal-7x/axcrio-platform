## 2. TAM — Total Addressable Market

**TAM (global, the long arc) ≈ $240B by 2030** — the AI-for-Sales-&-Marketing market
($58B in 2025 → $240.6B by 2030 @ 32.9% CAGR, MarketsandMarkets). This is the spend pool that shifts
from human sales/marketing labor + point tools to AI workforce software as the agentic wave lands.

**TAM (India, the beachhead) — bottom-up, the number that matters for our wedge:**

India has **65–70 million SMBs** (government data, cited by Meta), with **7.86 crore (78.6M) MSMEs
registered on the Udyam portal** as of Feb 2026 (PIB / IBEF) — of which ~6.5 crore are micro. The
realistic *digitally-reachable, growth-spending* SMB base (those who already advertise, run WhatsApp
Business, or employ telecallers) is the addressable core.

Two complementary bottom-up TAM constructions for India:

- **(a) "Replace the human go-to-market team."** A single in-house telecaller costs **₹17k–₹39k/month**
  (₹2–4.7 lakh/yr) loaded (Glassdoor/Indeed, 2025); most growth SMBs run 1–3 telecallers + at least
  one marketing person + ad budget. India's **call-centre/BPO market alone is ~$33B** and the
  customer-facing telemarketing/contact spend is large and growing (Ken Research; Grand View India
  call-and-contact-center outsourcing → $9.0B by 2030 @ 15.9%). The labor Famit automates is a
  multi-₹-lakh-crore annual wage pool across millions of SMBs.
- **(b) "Capture the marketing/ad + tooling spend."** India's **total ad market ≈ ₹2.0 lakh crore
  ($23B) in 2026**, with **digital at ~68% (₹1.1–1.4 lakh crore / $13–16B)** and growing ~19–22% YoY
  (Madison / Ipsos / Campaign Asia). >90% of businesses on Meta in India are MSMEs (Meta, Dec 2025).
  Add the SaaS/CRM/automation + messaging spend layered on top.

**India TAM (serviceable definition): ~10M digitally-active growth SMBs × ~$1,200/yr blended
platform value (subscription + usage + a slice of ad/labor spend they redirect) ≈ $12B/yr today,
expanding past $20B by 2030** as digital ad spend and AI adoption compound. (Bottom-up math in §4.)

## 3. SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

**SAM = the slice we can actually serve with the live product + near roadmap** — India-first,
SMBs that (i) already advertise on Meta/Google, (ii) use WhatsApp Business, and (iii) do outbound
phone follow-up. That triple-qualified segment is precisely where the closed-loop wins.

Anchors that bound it:
- **~15 million SMBs use WhatsApp Business in India**; **>2 lakh (200k+) SMBs already run
  Click-to-WhatsApp ads** (Meta / Gallabox / Storyboard18). CTWA is the **fastest-growing Meta ad
  format, +82% YoY** (Q1'25→Q1'26) and India is a top CTWA market.
- India has **~1–2 million active Meta advertisers** (industry estimate), >90% of them MSMEs.
- India conversational-AI market: **$455M (2024) → $1.85B (2030) @ 26.3%**; India voice-assistant:
  **$153M (2024) → $958M (2030) @ 35.7%** (Grand View / NextMSC) — the AI-voice layer of our SAM
  is itself a ~$1–2B India market by 2030.

**SAM (India, bottom-up): the ~2–4 million SMBs that advertise + use WhatsApp + do phone follow-up,
× ~$1,500–$2,400/yr realistic ARPU (they consolidate a telecaller seat + a messaging tool + ad
management) ≈ $4–7B/yr serviceable today**, growing with digital-ad and AI-voice adoption.
A defensible single number for the deck: **SAM ≈ $5B (India, today) → ~$9B by 2030.**

## 4. SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market + the bottom-up ARPU math

**SOM = what Famit can realistically capture in 3–5 years** as an early-stage, India-first entrant
landing the WhatsApp-+-voice-+-ads triple-qualified SMB. This is built bottom-up, not top-down %.

### The bottom-up ARPU model (the load-bearing math for the deck)

ARPU is built from what one growth SMB *already spends* that Famit consolidates:

| Spend line a growth SMB already pays | Today (per SMB / yr) | What Famit captures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 telecaller seat (loaded) | ₹2.5–4.7L ($3k–5.5k) | AI Telecaller replaces → platform fee + usage |
| WhatsApp Business / messaging tool + BSP | ₹30k–₹1L ($350–1,200) | native WhatsApp + AI builder |
| Ad management (agency or in-house ad-ops) | ₹50k–₹3L ($600–3,500) | autonomous ad ops + Growth-OS *(roadmap)* |
| CRM / marketing-automation SaaS | ₹20k–₹80k ($250–950) | replaced by the platform |
| **Total go-to-market spend Famit can address** | **₹3.5L–₹9L ($4k–11k)/yr** | — |

**Famit's blended ARPU target = ~₹1.2–2.0 lakh/yr ($1,400–2,400)** — a *fraction* of what the SMB
already spends, because we replace labor + several tools at once (the wedge is "cheaper than one
telecaller, does the work of the whole front office"). Outcome-priced upside (a slice of ad spend /
per-qualified-lead) lifts ARPU above subscription over time.

### SOM construction (India, 3–5 yr)

```
SAM (India, triple-qualified SMBs)         ≈ 2,500,000 SMBs
× realistic 3–5yr penetration              ≈ 1–2%      = 25,000–50,000 paying SMBs
× blended ARPU                             ≈ $1,800/yr
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SOM (India, 3–5 yr)                        ≈ $45M – $90M ARR
```

A credible **near-term SOM = $40–90M ARR in India within 3–5 years** from a low-single-% share of the
serviceable base. The $500M-ARR thesis (founder vision) is reached by (a) climbing ARPU via
outcome-pricing + Growth-OS ad % *(roadmap)*, (b) deeper penetration as the data-network-effect moat
compounds, and (c) the global expansion arc (§6) — not by needing a large share of any one market.

> **Honest framing for investors:** SOM is share-of-SAM × ARPU, kept deliberately conservative
> (1–2% penetration). The bull case is ARPU expansion, not implausible market share.

## 5. WHY NOW — the three tailwinds that make the timing non-negotiable

**Tailwind 1 — Meta Andromeda makes *signal quality* the only lever left (our exact moat).**
Meta rebuilt its ad-delivery stack around **Andromeda**, an AI retrieval engine (global rollout
completed Oct 2025) that is **~4× more efficient** at driving performance per unit of data/compute,
with a **10,000× model-capacity increase** and **+6% recall / +8% ad-quality** gains (Meta
Engineering). Targeting, bidding and placement are now done better by the platform than any human
buyer — so the *only* remaining advantage is **what conversion signal you feed it**. Famit owns the
ground-truth signal (call answered, lead qualified, booking, sale) and feeds quality-weighted
conversions back via CAPI — the **Revenue-Truth Signal Loop**. Andromeda turning the industry into a
"feed-it-better-signals" game is the single biggest validator of our flagship moat.

**Tailwind 2 — WhatsApp is becoming a paid, monetized, conversation-first channel — fast.**
Meta's **WhatsApp paid messaging crossed $2B annualized run-rate by Q4 2025** (FoA "other revenue"
$801M in Q4'25 alone, +59% YoY); **Click-to-WhatsApp ad spend grew +82% YoY** and CTWA is the
fastest-growing Meta format in WhatsApp-first markets (India, LATAM, SEA, MENA). India leads: **~535M
WhatsApp users, ~15M SMBs on WhatsApp Business, 200k+ on CTWA, ₹2.5 lakh-crore WhatsApp-commerce GMV
in 2025.** Per-message pricing (post-Jan-2026: marketing ≈ ₹0.78–0.88, utility/auth ≈ ₹0.11–0.15)
makes the *economics of automated conversations* a real, meterable line — exactly what Famit
orchestrates and bills on. The channel we're built on is the one Meta is monetizing hardest.

**Tailwind 3 — the AI-agent wave + India's multilingual-voice moment converge on our wedge.**
The AI-agents market is compounding at **43–46% CAGR ($8B→~$50B by 2030)**, and AI-for-sales-&-
marketing at **32.9% ($58B→$240B)**. In India specifically, multilingual voice is having its moment:
**70%+ of Indians speak non-English languages**, India voice-assistant market growing **35.7% CAGR**,
with Bhashini, Sarvam AI and Krutrim making vernacular voice production-grade. Famit's *live* real
AI multilingual tele-calling (96 real calls, 8 campaigns) rides exactly this curve — and India's
**$33B call-centre/BPO labor pool** is the cost base being automated. The technology, the channel
economics, and the labor-arbitrage all flip in our favor at the same moment.

## 6. GLOBAL EXPANSION ANGLE (the long arc)

The India playbook (WhatsApp-first, voice-first, multilingual, price-sensitive SMBs) ports directly
to the **WhatsApp-dominant emerging-market belt** — LATAM, Southeast Asia, MENA, Southern Europe —
the *same* markets Meta names as fastest-growing for CTWA. >200M businesses use WhatsApp Business
globally; >10M active Meta advertisers worldwide. Global TAM = the **$240B AI-for-sales-&-marketing
market by 2030**. The cross-tenant data-network-effect moat (anonymized priors by industry×geo×
objective) compounds with every new tenant in every new geo — a brand-new salon in any market starts
with the posterior of hundreds before it, which no single-geo incumbent can replicate.
