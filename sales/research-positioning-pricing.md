## 1. THE MARKET MAP — who we're really up against

Buyers today stitch together **3–4 separate tools + 2–3 humans** to do what Famit does in one loop.
That fragmentation IS our opening. Four competitor categories:

### 1.1 AI Voice pure-plays (sell *minutes*, not outcomes)
| Tool | What it is | Per-minute price (2025–26) | The gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ringg AI** (India) | AI voice agent, 10 Indian languages | **$0.10/min** flexible · **$0.06/min** at 100k+ min | Voice only. No WhatsApp follow-up, no ads, no closed loop. You still hire the rest. |
| **Bland AI** | All-inclusive US voice | **~$0.09/min** (tiered since Dec-2025) | US-first, weak Indian-language depth; voice silo. |
| **Vapi** | BYOK orchestration | **$0.05/min** platform fee + STT+LLM+TTS+telephony → real cost **$0.14–$0.33/min** | Developer toolkit — *you* assemble & maintain it. Not a business product. |
| **Retell AI** | Voice engine | **$0.07–$0.08/min** + LLM $0.006–$0.06/min | Same: a voice layer, not a revenue system. |

> **The voice-pure-play trap:** they bill you for *talk time* whether or not it converts. They have
> no idea what happened after the call. They can't follow up on WhatsApp, can't run the ad that
> sourced the lead, and can't feed the outcome back to Meta. **Famit's call is one hop in a loop;
> theirs is the whole product.**

### 1.2 WhatsApp tools (one channel, no voice, no ads)
| Tool (India) | Platform fee | Per-message markup | The gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AiSensy** | Free → ₹1,500 → ₹3,200/mo | ₹1.09/marketing template | WhatsApp blast tool. No AI calls, no ad loop, no booking. |
| **Wati** | ₹2,199 → ₹4,899 → ₹14,799/mo | + Meta msg cost | Same single channel. |
| **Interakt** | ₹3,499–₹10,499/quarter | ₹0.95–0.97/marketing msg | Same. (Meta base: ₹0.86/marketing msg from Jan-2026.) |

> WhatsApp tools are a *broadcast* layer. They don't dial, don't qualify by voice, don't run ads.
> Famit's WhatsApp is the **second hop** after the AI call — same lead, one memory, one loop.

### 1.3 All-in-one CRMs (systems of *record*, humans still do the work)
| Tool | Positioning | Price | The gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LeadSquared** | High-volume sales CRM + automation | Per-user, mid-market | A place to *store* leads. Humans still call & chase. |
| **Kylas** | India SMB CRM, flat-rate | **₹12,999/mo** unlimited users | Record-keeping, not a workforce. |
| **GoHighLevel** | Agency all-in-one (CRM+funnels+SMS) | ~$97–$497/mo | US-centric; weak Indian voice/WhatsApp; *you* still operate it. |
| **Groweon** | Budget India CRM | ₹2,999/mo / 3 users | Lead management + reminders. No AI does the work. |

> CRMs give you a **dashboard and a to-do list**. Famit gives you a **workforce that does the to-do
> list.** A CRM tells you to call lead #47; Famit already called lead #47, qualified them, sent the
> WhatsApp, and booked the site visit — and logged it in the CRM it also is.

### 1.4 The category nobody owns — the **Revenue-Truth Signal Loop** (our moat)
- Meta's **Offline Conversions API was discontinued May-2025 → folded into the Conversions API (CAPI)**.
  Industry-confirmed: when you feed *quality-weighted conversion values* back (form = low, appointment =
  higher, **paid customer = highest**), `maximize-conversion-value` bidding **lowers CPL and discovers
  new high-value audiences**. And: **timing is critical** — waiting 90 days to confirm a lead means the
  platform already wasted weeks optimizing for junk.
- **No competitor owns the post-click conversation layer.** Voice tools don't touch ads. Ad tools don't
  call. CRMs don't bid. **Famit is the only one that sees the call outcome + the WhatsApp reply + the
  booking + the sale — and pushes that truth back to Meta/Google in near-real-time** so the ad engine
  starts hunting for people who **answer calls and buy**, not people who just fill forms.
- This is the **GROWTH OS** flagship: the vendor's own voice + WhatsApp outcomes become the highest-
  quality conversion signal a small business can possibly send an ad platform — a signal no point-tool
  can produce because **only the owner of the whole loop has the data.**

## 4. PRICING MODEL (India-relevant, anchored to "replace a ₹20–40k/mo telecaller team")

### 4.1 The anchor (the number every tier is measured against)
Verified India salary data (2025): a telecaller executive averages **~₹39,000/mo**; general telecallers
**~₹17,000/mo**; range **₹23k–₹1.15L/mo**. A *small team* of 2–3 callers + a part-time marketing person =
**₹60k–₹1.2L/month in salaries alone** — before incentives, attrition, training, dialer, SIM, and the
WhatsApp + ad tools on top.

> **The anchor line:** *"One telecaller costs you ₹20–40k a month, takes leave, quits, and calls 40
> leads a day in one language. Famit costs less, never sleeps, calls every lead in their own language,
> follows up on WhatsApp, and gets your ads cheaper. Replace the team — keep the closers."*

### 4.2 The model — **platform subscription + usage credits (prepaid wallet)**
Two-part pricing that maps to the live billing engine:
1. **Monthly platform fee** (the workforce, the modules, the seats) — predictable.
2. **Usage credits** (prepaid wallet, INR paise-metered) for the *variable* AI work — voice minutes,
   WhatsApp messages, AI banners, ad-creative gen. **You pay for outcomes used, not seats idling.**
   Credits map 1:1 to the live wallet (`wallet_accounts`, idempotent, no-double-spend, audited).
   Indicative consumption (real costs): **AI banner ≈ ₹10**, **WhatsApp marketing msg ≈ ₹0.86 + small
   margin**, **voice ≈ paise-metered per connected minute** (priced to undercut Ringg's $0.10/min while
   bundling the follow-up + ads the voice tools can't).

### 4.4 Optional outcome-aligned add-on (the long-game wedge)
Because Famit owns the *whole loop* (uniquely able to attribute revenue), a future **performance tier**
is credible: a lower base fee + a small **% of attributed booked revenue** or **per-qualified-appointment**
fee. No point-tool can offer this — they can't see the outcome. Flag as **Phase-2 pricing**, not launch.

## 5. THE ROI STORY + INTERACTIVE CALCULATOR SPEC

### 5.1 The narrative (what the calculator proves)
> *"You're already spending on leads and on people to chase them. Famit doesn't add a cost — it
> **replaces** one (the telecaller salary) and **multiplies** another (every lead actually gets called,
> in their language, within minutes, then followed up — so more of the leads you already pay for turn
> into customers). Here's your number."*

Three value levers the calculator computes:
1. **Salary replaced** — the telecaller/team cost Famit removes.
2. **Speed + coverage uplift** — Famit calls **100% of leads within minutes**, in-language, with WhatsApp
   follow-up. Humans reach maybe 40–60% and let the rest go cold. More contacted → more qualified → more
   booked. (Lead-response speed lifting conversion is well-established; we let the user set their own uplift.)
3. **Cheaper leads over time** — the Signal Loop feeds *paid-customer* outcomes back to Meta/Google, so
   `maximize-conversion-value` bidding drives CPL down (industry-documented). Modeled as an optional
   second-stage saving (conservative, user-adjustable).

### 5.2 Calculator INPUTS (what the prospect types)
| # | Input | Field type | Sensible default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Leads per month | number | 500 | total inbound leads they currently get |
| I2 | Current cost per qualified lead (₹) | number | 400 | their blended CPL today (ad spend ÷ qualified leads) |
| I3 | Telecaller / team monthly salary cost (₹) | number | 30,000 | the human cost Famit replaces (per-caller × heads) |
| I4 | Number of telecallers/staff replaced | number | 1 | multiplies I3 |
| I5 | Current lead→customer conversion rate (%) | number | 5 | how many leads become paying customers today |
| I6 | Average revenue per customer (₹) | number | 15,000 | order value / deal size |
| I7 | Expected conversion uplift with Famit (%) | slider 0–100 | 25 | *relative* lift (e.g. 5% → 6.25% at 25% uplift). User-owned assumption. |
| I8 | Famit plan fee (₹/mo) | preset/number | 24,999 | from the chosen tier |
| I9 | (optional) Expected CPL reduction from Signal Loop (%) | slider 0–40 | 10 | conservative; off by default |

### 5.3 Calculator FORMULAS (what the proposal computes live)

```
# --- Baseline (today, without Famit) ---
customers_now        = I1 * (I5/100)                      # leads × conv rate
revenue_now          = customers_now * I6                  # current monthly revenue from these leads
salary_cost          = I3 * I4                             # human cost being replaced

# --- With Famit ---
conv_famit_pct       = I5 * (1 + I7/100)                   # uplifted conversion rate
customers_famit      = I1 * (conv_famit_pct/100)
revenue_famit        = customers_famit * I6

extra_customers      = customers_famit - customers_now
extra_revenue        = revenue_famit - revenue_now         # NEW revenue from the same leads

# --- Optional: cheaper leads via the Signal Loop ---
cpl_saving           = (I1 * I2) * (I9/100)                # ad-spend efficiency saved (if enabled)
# (I1*I2 ≈ current monthly qualified-lead spend; I9% of it saved)

# --- Net monthly value ---
famit_cost           = I8                                  # plan fee (credits usage shown separately/included)
monthly_gain         = extra_revenue + salary_cost + cpl_saving - famit_cost
#                      ^new revenue   ^salary you stop paying  ^cheaper leads   ^what Famit costs

roi_multiple         = (extra_revenue + salary_cost + cpl_saving) / famit_cost   # e.g. "7.2× return"
roi_pct              = (monthly_gain / famit_cost) * 100
payback_days         = 30 * famit_cost / max(1, (extra_revenue + salary_cost + cpl_saving))
annual_gain          = monthly_gain * 12
```

**Headline outputs to render big:**
- **₹ Net monthly gain** (`monthly_gain`)
- **ROI multiple** (`roi_multiple`, e.g. "Every ₹1 → ₹7.2")
- **Extra customers/month** (`extra_customers`)
- **Payback period** (`payback_days`, usually "< 1 week")
- **Annual value created** (`annual_gain`)

**Worked example (defaults above, GROWTH tier, Signal Loop off):**
- customers_now = 500 × 5% = **25** ; revenue_now = 25 × ₹15,000 = ₹3,75,000
- conv_famit = 5% × 1.25 = 6.25% → customers_famit = **31.25** ; revenue_famit = ₹4,68,750
- extra_revenue = **₹93,750** ; salary replaced = ₹30,000 ; famit_cost = ₹24,999
- **monthly_gain = 93,750 + 30,000 − 24,999 = ₹98,751** · **ROI ≈ 4.95×** · **payback ≈ 6 days** ·
  **annual_gain ≈ ₹11.85L**

> Calculator UX rules: every assumption is **user-editable** (especially I7/I9 — never hard-sell a fake
> uplift; let them dial it to 0 and *still* see salary-replacement value go positive). Show a toggle for
> the Signal Loop saving so the base case stands on salary + conversion alone. Label assumptions clearly
> ("your estimate"). The proof of credibility is that **the floor case — uplift 0, loop off — still
> wins on the replaced salary minus the lower plan fee.**
