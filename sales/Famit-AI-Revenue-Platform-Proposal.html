Safe to say yes

Built to spend your money like it's ours.

A system that calls customers and spends on ads has to earn trust by design. Here's how the risk is engineered out.

PIN + risk firewall

Every risky action — spend, bulk-send, launch ads — is risk-classified and gated with a PIN step-up. Wrong PIN is denied and audited.

Immutable audit ledger

Every AI decision is recorded append-only with its reason. No double-spend — proven under 24 concurrent transactions.

Bank-grade data isolation

Account identity from the token (never the request body) plus Postgres Row-Level Security. A leaked token still can't read another business's data.

Compliant by default

DND, consent, calling windows and India's DPDP are enforced in the dial path — not an optional setting. The AI never invents a price, claim or RERA number.
